## 🎛 Featured Projects

### Phase Align 🔗 https://github.com/tehguitarist/PhaseAlign

A CPU light tool to lock in multi-mic sources, DI/Amp situations, or any time you need to fix the phase between two tracks. Even has an analysis mode to help get you there faster as a bonus!

---

### Tommy 🔗 https://github.com/tehguitarist/Tommy

A transparent, touch-sensitive overdrive plugin inspired by the classic Timmy pedal. Circuit modeled, not profiled or captured.

---

### Monarch of Tone 🔗 https://github.com/tehguitarist/Monarch-of-Tone

A dual-channel boutique overdrive inspired by Bluesbreaker-style circuits also known as the King of Tone. This one doesn't have a 6 year waiting list either! Circuit modeled, not profiled or captured.

---

### Echo Pre 3 🔗 https://github.com/tehguitarist/EchoPre3

A one knob, 3 EQ voiced pedal based on the revered EchoPlex EP-3 preamp - inspired by boutique pedals like the Chase Tone Secret Pre. This is just straight up secret sauce. Circuit modeled, not profiled or captured.

---
### NoAmp Low Rider DI 🔗 https://github.com/tehguitarist/NoAmp-Low-Rider-DI

All three revisions of the legendary Bass Driver DI, circuit modeled and ready to add some clank to your bass.

---

### Obsidian B7000 🔗 https://github.com/tehguitarist/Obsidian-B7000

When you need something different from a BDDI, there is the Microtubes B7K Ultra. This is a circuit modeled plugin of that exact pedal.

### BPM2Time 🔗 https://github.com/tehguitarist/BPM2Time

A simple utility for converting musical tempo into practical delay and timing values.

### Note2Freq 🔗 https://github.com/tehguitarist/Note2Freq

A musician-friendly note frequency reference.

### Board Game Helper 🔗 https://apps.apple.com/us/app/board-game-helper/id6541754469
